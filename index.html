FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What products does TI-COM sell?

TI-COM deals heavily in networking modules, SFP and SFP+ modules, optical transceivers, fiber optic products, switches, routers, Ethernet/network cables, connectors and networking accessories.

How can I place an order?

Add products to the Product Enquiry cart and send the enquiry through WhatsApp. You can also contact TI-COM directly.

Can I pay by bank transfer?

Yes. The website provides the stated Moniepoint bank-transfer details. Payment confirmation is handled manually through WhatsApp.

How do I confirm my payment?

After transfer, use the WhatsApp payment-confirmation button and send your payment confirmation and order details.

Where is TI-COM located?

Lagos Island, Lagos State, Nigeria.

Can I contact TI-COM through WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp is used for product enquiries, cart enquiries, payment confirmation and general contact.

How can I visit the physical store?

Use the Google Maps directions feature toward Lagos Island, Lagos State, Nigeria. The website does not invent a street address.
